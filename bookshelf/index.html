2. Subscribed: Why the Subscription Model Will Be Your Company’s Future―and What to Do About It by Tien Tzuo (📘 Product & Business)#

“Subscribed” makes a compelling case that we’re shifting from an ownership economy to a subscription economy, where businesses need to obsess not over one-time sales, but over long-term relationships with their customers.

Key Takeaways#

  • From Product to Service: Every product today can be sold as a service—cars, software, even exercise bikes.
  • Customer Lifetime Value > One-Time Revenue: Subscriptions flip the business focus from acquisition to ongoing retention and value.
  • New Metrics Matter: Think MRR, churn, net dollar retention—not just revenue and profit.
  • It’s Not Just SaaS: While pioneered by software, industries like automotive, media, and fitness are rapidly shifting too.
  • Org Structure Must Change: Sales, finance, product, and support need to align around customer success.

👉 Buy on Amazon
