Problem Statement#

Text-based job descriptions are often bland and fail to convey the culture or human side of a workplace. JobCulture proposes a video-first platform where companies can better market their roles, team, and values—while job seekers gain deeper insight and connection.

What is JobCulture?#

A video-first job marketplace where:

  • Companies post video job descriptions instead of just text.
  • Candidates view videos that showcase role expectations, company culture, and real team members.
  • The platform facilitates better job discovery and screening, with the option for candidates to respond via short video submissions.

Competitor: Ongig

Why Video?#

  • 📈 27x more likely to get clicks than text ads
  • 🔁 1,200% more shares than combined text and images
  • 🧠 95% message retention with video (vs. 10% from text)
  • 💬 +97% purchase/job interest from a positive video experience

Personas#

🎯 B2C – Job Seekers#

Jay : Tired of lifeless job descriptions and hunting down culture clues across sites.

Gayle: Wants a central place to understand role fit and team/company vibe.

💡 Solution: Video-based job ads that highlight workspaces, values, and team dynamics — beyond bullet points.

👩‍💼 B2B – Recruiters#

Neha: Aims to boost engagement and conversion.

Roger: Seeks behavior & soft-skill insight in remote screening.

💡 Solution:

  • Ask candidates to submit video responses.
  • Showcase company culture through employer videos.
  • Drive better candidate-job match quality.

Wireframes Overview#

  • 🧭 Seamless onboarding: Sign up/login flow
  • 📺 Scrollable video feed of job posts
  • 🔍 Deeper info via swipe
  • 📝 Apply with ease; see more roles at the same company
  • 🎥 Upload video answers for screening questions

Monetization Model#

💰 Freemium core, with 3 revenue channels:

  1. Talent Solutions (55%)
    • Recruiter tools for hiring & L&D
  2. Marketing Solutions (25%)
    • Sponsored posts and display ads
  3. Premium Subscriptions (17%)
    • Extra visibility, outreach, and sales enablement tools

Key Metrics (for Hiring Managers)#

  • ⏱️ Time on page
  • 📥 Apply rate
  • ✅ Hire ratio
  • 🎯 Quality of hire
  • 📡 Channel effectiveness
  • 🚀 Time to hire

Total Addressable Market#

  • 🌎 5.9B+ potential workers
  • 🏢 300M+ companies worldwide
  • 📲 33M monthly users
  • 🎥 450M+ weekly impressions
  • 🎯 14M+ companies on platform

Final Thoughts#

JobCulture reimagines how candidates and companies connect—through culture-first storytelling. As hiring becomes increasingly remote and human-centric, video job descriptions aren’t just a feature; they’re the future.
