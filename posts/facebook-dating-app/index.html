📌 Problem Statement#

Should Facebook enter the dating space with a standalone app? If yes, what are the metrics for success, and how should it execute to differentiate itself in a competitive market?

🔍 Why This Strategy?#

  • Facebook’s mission is to bring people closer, and dating is a natural extension.
  • Leveraging Facebook’s social graph, events, and interest groups could create unmatched matchmaking capabilities.
  • A standalone app prevents Facebook’s core product from being diluted.

🔍 Summary#

InstaDate is a proposed standalone dating app by Facebook, designed to leverage its social graph and community ecosystem to match users based on mutual friends, interests, and events.

Key highlights:

  • Uses Facebook’s strengths: data, network, and engagement.
  • Adopts a freemium revenue model.
  • Competes with Tinder and Bumble.
  • Focuses on event-driven and interest-based matching.
