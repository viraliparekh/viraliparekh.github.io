👋 Hi, I’m Virali Parekh — a Product Manager passionate about building user-first, data-backed, and impact-driven products.

Currently, I’m leading user engagement, product adoption, and operational efficiency at A23 Rummy (Head Digital Works) — one of India’s top real-money gaming platforms with over 4 million monthly active users. From ideating features like Leaderboards and PlayPass, to optimizing tournaments and customer support automation, I’ve worked across the 0→1 and 1→10 stages of product growth.

Before this, I built engaging multiplayer features in Pool Champs (MPL) and improved learning outcomes in Kiddobox, a preschool edtech app — working hands-on with LiveOps, game mechanics, and first-time user experience (FTUE).

  • A track record of shipping features that improve monetization, conversion, and engagement
  • Hands-on with product analytics (Google Analytics, MixPanel, CleverTap), SQL, and A/B testing
  • Strong cross-functional execution across design, tech, customer support, and marketing
